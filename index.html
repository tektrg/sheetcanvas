SheetCanvas: an agent-native spreadsheet canvas

SheetCanvas is an agent-native spreadsheet canvas for analysts, operators, and founders that puts spreadsheets, charts, pivots, notes, and diagrams side by side on one canvas, and lets your own AI agent (such as Claude or Codex, connected over MCP) build them from your files and data connectors.

Published October 20, 2026 · Updated October 20, 2026 · Features as of Oct 2026

What is SheetCanvas and what can it do?

  • Imports local CSV, XLSX, and XLS files as spreadsheet objects on a canvas.
  • Builds bar, line, area, pie, scatter, treemap, pivot, and sparkline views next to the source data.
  • Keeps rich-text notes and diagrams (Excalidraw-style drawings and Mermaid) beside the evidence they explain.
  • Exposes the open canvas to external agents over MCP (Model Context Protocol), so Claude, Codex, or another MCP client can read, filter, sort, and chart sheets on the canvas. (A built-in copilot is announced in the app as coming soon.)
  • Connects to ClickHouse, Google Sheets, and Google Analytics 4 (GA4) as refreshable connected sheets.
  • Publishes a note or page to a public link, and embeds individual charts or pages on other websites.

Who is SheetCanvas for?

  • Analysts and operators who explore CSV, Excel, or warehouse exports and need the source table, the chart, and the conclusion visible together.
  • Founders and small teams who want a quick dashboard from Google Analytics, Google Sheets, or ClickHouse without setting up a BI tool.
  • People who work with AI agents who want Claude, Codex, or another MCP client to leave durable tables and charts instead of a chat reply that scrolls away.

Frequently asked questions

What is SheetCanvas?
SheetCanvas is a browser-based spreadsheet canvas. Instead of one grid with tabs, you place spreadsheets, charts, pivots, notes, and diagrams on a single canvas so the data and the explanation stay visible together. MCP access lets your AI agent build those objects for you.
Who is SheetCanvas for?
It is for analysts, operators, founders, and small teams who explore exported or connected business data and want a visual working surface, and for people who want their AI agent to produce durable tables and charts rather than a chat answer.
Is SheetCanvas a replacement for Excel or Google Sheets?
No. Excel and Google Sheets are deeper spreadsheet systems for complex formulas, macros, and long-lived shared workbooks. SheetCanvas is better when spreadsheet data needs to sit beside charts, notes, and diagrams on one canvas during analysis. Many people use both.
Which data sources does SheetCanvas connect to?
As of October 2026, SheetCanvas imports local CSV, XLSX, and XLS files and has connectors for ClickHouse, Google Sheets, and Google Analytics 4. It does not claim connectors beyond those.
Can I use SheetCanvas with Claude or Codex?
Yes. SheetCanvas exposes the open canvas as an MCP server. Generate a connection URL from the Connect Agent dialog, add it to an MCP-capable client such as Claude or Codex, and the agent can list sheets, read ranges, and create charts while the SheetCanvas tab is open.
Where is my data stored?
Workspaces are stored locally in your browser. Local file imports stay on your device. Connector credentials are stored encrypted by the SheetCanvas backend so it can query the source on your behalf, and published pages are stored on the server only when you choose to publish.
How much does SheetCanvas cost?
As of October 2026, SheetCanvas has no published pricing. The web app opens at sheetcanvas.com and you can start working without creating an account.
