Terms

Terms for using SheetCanvas.

SheetCanvas is a live browser app for local spreadsheet files, canvas-based analysis objects, and shipped connector-backed sheets. These terms describe practical usage boundaries for the current product.

Last reviewed: May 27, 2026

Use of the app

Use SheetCanvas for analysis you can verify.

The app helps you arrange tables, charts, pivots, sparklines, and notes on a spreadsheet canvas. You are responsible for checking the imported data, connector output, formulas, charts, and conclusions before relying on them.

  • Local files: only import CSV, XLSX, or XLS files that you have the right to use in this browser-based workspace.
  • Connector access: only connect Google Analytics or ClickHouse sources that you are authorized to access.
  • Backups: keep your own source files and records. SheetCanvas should not be your only copy of important data.

Product boundaries

SheetCanvas is not every spreadsheet, database, or BI workflow.

No universal replacement claim

SheetCanvas is useful for spatial spreadsheet analysis, but it does not replace mature spreadsheet macros, full BI governance, or collaborative database workflows. The Excel, Google Sheets, and Airtable comparisons explain those boundaries.

Connector guardrails

Connector-backed sheets depend on configured backend services and source-system permissions. ClickHouse query usage should follow the read-only guardrails in the connector docs.

Availability

The product may change as SheetCanvas evolves.

Features, connectors, and support pages may be revised as the product changes. Product-sensitive pages include visible reviewed dates so you can tell when the public guidance was last checked.
