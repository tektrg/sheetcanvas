SheetCanvas vs Excel

SheetCanvas is a free visual canvas for spreadsheets, charts and notes, where you analyze data and publish shareable data pages. Microsoft Excel is a different kind of tool; this page compares them by use case.

Published October 20, 2026 · Last reviewed: October 20, 2026 · Facts as of Oct 2026

Which is better for data analysis, SheetCanvas or Excel?

It depends on whether the workbook is the product or the analysis is. Choose Excel for financial models, complex formulas, VBA macros, Power Query pipelines, and files your organization already standardizes on; Excel is the deeper and more compatible spreadsheet. Choose SheetCanvas when you are exploring an export and want the source table, several charts, a pivot, and written notes visible side by side on one canvas, or when you want an AI agent to build that layout for you over MCP. A common pattern is to keep Excel as the system of record and open an exported CSV or XLSX in SheetCanvas to explain it.

Frequently asked questions

Can SheetCanvas open Excel files?
Yes. SheetCanvas imports XLSX and XLS files, as well as CSV, as spreadsheet objects on the canvas.
Does SheetCanvas support VBA macros?
No. SheetCanvas does not run VBA or Office Scripts. Use Excel for macro-driven workbooks.
When should I stay in Excel?
Stay in Excel for complex financial models, macro automation, Power Query transformations, and any file that must behave exactly like an Excel workbook for colleagues.
