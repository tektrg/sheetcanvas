Privacy

Privacy notes for the SheetCanvas app.

SheetCanvas is a browser-based spreadsheet canvas for local files, visual analysis objects, and connector-backed sheets. This page explains the product-specific data paths that matter when you open the app.

Last reviewed: May 27, 2026

Open SheetCanvas Read setup docs

Local files

Local spreadsheet imports start in your browser.

When you import a CSV, XLSX, or XLS file, SheetCanvas turns it into a canvas table object for analysis in the app. The SEO pages and app shell do not require account creation before you can inspect a local file.

  • Workspace data: tables, charts, pivots, sparklines, and notes are part of the SheetCanvas workspace you create in the browser.
  • File contents: do not put sensitive or regulated data into SheetCanvas unless the current product behavior and your own policies are appropriate for that data.
  • Browser storage: if the app stores workspace state locally, clearing browser data may remove that local state.

Connected sheets

Connectors use backend flows where setup is required.

Google Analytics

The GA4 connector uses an OAuth-backed flow through the configured backend. Use it only with properties you are allowed to access, and review the Google Analytics connector guide before connecting production reporting data.

ClickHouse

The ClickHouse connector is designed around read-only query workflows. Use single read-only queries that match the ClickHouse connector guide and avoid credentials or queries that expose data you do not intend to analyze in SheetCanvas.

Site analytics

SheetCanvas uses site analytics to understand product discovery.

The public app shell includes Google Analytics measurement for page and product discovery reporting. This is separate from the Google Analytics connector, which is for bringing your GA4 reporting rows into a connected sheet.

SheetCanvas is built by theindie.app.
